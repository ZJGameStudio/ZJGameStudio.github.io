How we work

Four stages. No surprises three sprints in.

The same process runs whether the build takes six weeks or six months — only the depth of each stage changes.

01 — Week 1

Prototype

We build the core loop first, on paper if we have to, until we know the game is fun before a single asset exists.

02 — Weeks 2–3

Direction

Art, systems, and platform locked against the brief, not against whatever we shipped last time.

03 — Weeks 4–8

Build

Design and engineering sit in the same sprint. What you playtest in review is what ships, not a mockup.

04 — Ongoing

Launch & live-ops

Certification, store pages, and patch support your team can run with after we hand over the build.

The studio

Small on purpose. Every project gets the full team.

We turn down more work than we take. It's the only way nine people stay this involved in every case.

ZJGameStudio started as three friends prototyping on a shared laptop in Brooklyn. Nine years on, we're still a studio, not a contractor pool — no producer layer between you and the people building the game, and no title passed off once the pitch is won.
